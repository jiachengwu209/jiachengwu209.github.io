## About Me

I am a PhD researcher in Chemical Engineering at the University of Sydney.

My research focuses on practical high-mass-loading carbon electrodes for electrochemical energy storage, particularly aqueous zinc-ion hybrid capacitors and sodium-ion batteries. My work investigates electronic and ionic transport in thick porous electrodes, electrode architecture, carbon nanomaterials, and practical energy-storage devices.

## Research

### High-Mass-Loading Carbon Electrodes for Zinc-Ion Hybrid Capacitors

My PhD research focuses on understanding and overcoming the transport limitations of practical thick carbon electrodes.

Key topics include:

- Electronic transport in thick carbon electrodes
- Zn²⁺ ion transport and diffusion
- Electrode porosity and tortuosity
- CNT conductive networks
- Directional pore engineering
- Electrolyte wettability
- Practical coin-cell and pouch-cell configurations

### Sodium-Ion Batteries

I am also interested in sustainable hard-carbon anodes for sodium-ion batteries, including biomass-derived hard carbon and rapid Joule-heating synthesis.

### Carbon Nanomaterials

My current research interests also include porous carbon nanofibers, electrospinning, pore engineering, and advanced carbon architectures for electrochemical energy storage.
